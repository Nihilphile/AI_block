# External Decision Brief

Return:

1. exact question and blocked decision;
2. finding;
3. authoritative evidence and applicable version/date;
4. documented guarantee versus observation/inference;
5. compatibility limits and conflicting evidence;
6. remaining uncertainty;
7. construction implication without making the Orchestrator's product decision.

Do not expand into a general technology survey.
